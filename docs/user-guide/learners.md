# Guide for learners

How to take your training in the Maxx Sales Training portal.

## Signing in

1. Go to the training portal and choose **Create an account** the first time. Enter your full name as it should appear on your certificates, your work email and a password of at least 6 characters.
2. If asked, confirm your email from the message you receive, then sign in.
3. Use **Sign in** next time. To sign out, click your initials in the top-right corner and choose **Sign out**.
4. **Forgot your password?** On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**, enter your email and click **Send reset link**. Open the link in the email on the same computer or phone, then choose a new password.

## Your courses

After signing in you see your courses as tiles. Type in the search box at the top to find a course by name or code.

- **Required courses** are the ones assigned to you. Your company tracks whether you've completed them.
- **Other courses** are optional. You can take them, but they don't count toward your requirements.

Each tile shows where you are:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not started | You haven't opened the course yet |
| In progress | You've started the reading |
| Not passed yet | You've taken the assessment but not passed yet |
| Completed | You've finished the course (a large green tick also appears) |

The colour at the top of each tile matches its badge: grey for not started, orange for in progress, red for not passed yet, green for completed. The tile lists the steps with a tick next to each one you've finished. The orange button always takes you to the next step: **Start course**, **Continue reading**, **Take assessment** or **View certificate**.

## Step 1: Reading

1. Read the course PDF in the portal. Use the arrows to move between pages and **−** / **+** to zoom. **Open PDF ↗** opens it in a new tab.
2. Some courses have several documents ("decks"). They appear as numbered tabs. You need to open every one.
3. As you scroll, **reading questions** appear at certain pages. Answer each one correctly to keep going. If you get one wrong, read the explanation and try again.
4. Each course has a **minimum reading time**. The panel on the right shows your time, reading questions and decks. The timer pauses while a reading question is open.
5. When everything is done, click **Start assessment →**. For a reading-only course, click **Finish and get certificate →**.

If you leave and come back, the reading questions you've already answered stay answered, but the decks you opened and the on-screen timer start again.

## Step 2: Assessment

1. Answer every question, then click **Submit assessment**. If you leave before submitting, you'll get the same questions when you come back.
2. To pass you need **at least the passing score** (usually 80%) **and** every question marked **Mandatory** right.
3. You'll see your score and a review of every question, with the correct answer and an explanation.

### If you don't pass

- You have **3 attempts**. Each retake has a fresh set of questions.
- After 3 unsuccessful attempts the assessment locks. Your admins are told automatically and can approve 3 more. Go back over the course material in the meantime.

## Step 3: Certificate

The first time you pass a course (or finish a reading-only course), you get a certificate. Open it from the results screen, the course card, or your profile. Use **Download PDF** to save it or **Open to print** to print it. Certificates show a "valid until" date two years after completion.

## Your profile

Click your initials → **View profile** to see:

- your required training and what's still outstanding;
- the training groups you're in;
- your certificates;
- every assessment attempt, with a **Review** of your answers.

## Questions?

If a course is missing, an assessment is locked, or your name is wrong on a certificate, contact your training admin.
